✨ What is Swarlipi?

Swarlipi is a powerful yet simple application designed for musicians, students, and teachers of Hindustani classical music. It lets you write Bhatkhande music notation as real Unicode text in your preferred script - whether that's Gurmukhi, Devanagari, Bengali, Gujarati, or Latin.

The most amazing part? You don't need any special notation font! Every symbol, line, and mark you see in traditional Bhatkhande notation is drawn using CSS and SVG technology. This means your notations will look perfect on any device, anywhere - they're just regular text that you can copy, paste, print, or share with anyone.

🔍 Understanding Bhatkhande Notation

Bhatkhande notation is the standard system for writing Hindustani classical music. Developed by Pandit Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande, it uses the syllables Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni to represent notes. Swarlipi makes this system completely digital, giving you precise control over every mark and symbol.

With Swarlipi, you can represent:

  • Basic notes (swaras)
  • Octave indicators - dots above and below notes
  • Timing marks - matra divisions
  • Ornaments (gamakas)
  • Meends - glides between notes
  • And much more!

The beauty of Swarlipi is that all these complex symbols become real, copyable Unicode text that automatically renders correctly on any modern device.

🌐 Why Multiple Scripts Matter

Indian classical music is practiced and taught across the entire subcontinent. Different regions prefer different writing systems:

  • Gurmukhi - Used primarily in Punjab and among Sikh communities
  • Devanagari - Standard in Hindi-speaking regions and for Sanskrit texts
  • Bengali - Essential for the rich musical traditions of Bengal
  • Gujarati - Used in Gujarat and among Gujarati musical traditions
  • Latin - For international students and those familiar with Western notation

Swarlipi's multi-script support means you can learn or teach in the script that's most comfortable for you, or even switch scripts mid-document to help students connect their knowledge.
